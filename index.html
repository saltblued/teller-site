Teller

Support

Teller is a paper fortune teller for iPhone. Pick a corner, pick a number, lift the flap.

Get in touch

Questions, a bug, an idea: write to isabluedesign@gmail.com. I read everything.

I bought the unlock. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Teller, tap Unlock, then Already bought it? Restore. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I don't hear any sound.

Teller follows your iPhone's Silent mode. Turn Silent mode off, turn the volume up, and check that Sound is on in Teller's Settings.

Where did my teller go?

A teller you create is kept only once you tap Save under an answer (saving comes with the unlock). An unsaved teller is deleted when you switch to another one, or when you close the app.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Teller. It answers from the last teller you folded.

How do I delete or reorder tellers?

With the unlock, open the gallery and touch and hold any teller.
