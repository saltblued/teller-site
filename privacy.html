Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Teller collects nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and Teller never sends anything you make to us or to anyone else.

What you make stays on your phone. The tellers you write, the corners you draw, your paper and your settings are kept in Teller's own storage on your device. If you back up your phone to iCloud or a computer, they go into that backup along with the rest of the app. Deleting Teller deletes them.

Widgets read the teller you last folded from the same device. Nothing leaves it.

Dictation. When you tap the microphone, Teller asks for the microphone and Speech Recognition, and uses Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into your options. On phones that can, this happens entirely on the device. On others, Apple processes the audio under Apple's own privacy policy. Teller keeps no recordings.

The unlock is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment. Teller only learns whether the unlock is owned.

Sharing happens only when you tap Share, through your phone's own share sheet, to wherever you choose.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and in the app. Questions can be sent through the support link on Teller's App Store page.
